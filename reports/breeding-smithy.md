## Buck Snapshot
Smithy is a useful young `VVE 86` for frame and dairyness, limited by narrow Rump Width (24) and intermediate Rump Angle (28). He is a situational buck, not the default for narrow does or for does whose liked flatter angles need protecting. On steep does he still flattens somewhat (less than Michael); on Snickers/Tinkles he steepens liked flatter angles. BIS ranks Snickers highest for him because dairyness GapClosure is large — that does **not** erase the angle/width risks called out below.
