## Breeding Implications / Recommendation

### How to choose among daughters (priority order)

1. **Rump Width** — keep the widest; cull-lean or sell the narrowest. This is the trait both parents fail; sisters will differ, and that difference is the selection opportunity.
2. **Dairyness** — prefer daughters clearly sharper / more dairy than Snickers. A capacious but coarse daughter repeats the dam’s main scorecard hole.
3. **Rump Angle** — prefer **30–35**. Avoid keepers flatter than the dam (above 36). Steep is less likely from this pair but still less ideal than preferred-band.
4. **After freshening (decide final keepers then if possible)**  
   - **Medial Suspensory Ligament** — require clear improvement over dam 18; weak cleft → do not retain as a herd doe.  
   - **Teat Placement** — prefer closer than dam 20; still-wide with weak MSL → hard pass.  
   - Protect **Fore Udder Attachment** / rear-udder quality if present.
5. **Strength / Body Depth** — expect both; use as tie-breakers only after width, dairy, angle, and mammary.
6. **Rear Legs Side View** — note posty extremes; low-h² — don’t over-weight vs mammary/width, but prefer intermediate when sisters are otherwise equal.

### Practical keep / pass shorthand

| Keep if… | Pass / sell if… |
|----------|-----------------|
| Widest rump in the sister set **and** dairy vs dam | Narrowest rumps, especially with level/flat angle |
| RA in or near 30–35 | Flatter than Snickers (RA > 36) |
| At freshening: MSL and TP better than dam | MSL still weak + teats still wide |
| Capacious without coarseness | Deep but coarse / low-dairy like the dam |

### Use of keepers

- Best daughters: breed next to bucks that add **width and proven MSL / closer teat placement** (outside mammary genetics, or Finale only as a partial estimated MSL lift — not a teat-placement solution). Prefer **Michael** over repeating Smithy if width remains binding.
- Do **not** stack another narrow, unproven-mammary sire on a narrow daughter from this pair.
- Long-term: treat this family as a **dairyness / capacity line that still needs width + mammary imported** — selection among these sisters is the first filter, not the last fix.

### Bottom line

Rank sisters for **width first, dairyness second, rump angle third**; lock final herd spots only after **MSL and teat placement** are seen in milk. The Smithy × Snickers pedigree will not hand you mammary or width — you have to select it.
