## Outside Genetics — Biggest Herd Levers

Herd-wide binding gaps the on-hand bucks do **not** solve cleanly: **Medial Suspensory Ligament** (doe range 13–18) and **Teat Placement** (17–21, wide). Michael and Smithy are unscored for mammary; Finale’s estimate is only intermediate MSL (~23.5) and still-wide TP (~21).

**Outside buck (preferred)** — look for transmitting evidence (own daughters or strong dam/sister mammary) with:
1. **Medial Suspensory Ligament** clearly above intermediate — target daughters/means roughly **≥28–32** (herd floor is 13–18)
2. **Teat Placement** closer / less wide — target roughly **≥25–30** (herd is 17–21)
3. Hold or add **Dairyness** (≥30) and **Rump Width** (≥28) so Snickers/Amber-type gaps are not reopened
4. **Rump Angle** that moves animals toward **30–35** (not a flattener for Snickers/Tinkles; not irrelevant for steep does)
5. Prefer proven mammary over another unproven “dairy/width” sire — those levers are already covered on-hand

**Outside doe (optional):** A mid/high-80s+ doe with **strong MSL and closer teat placement** plus dairyness would add dams that do not stack the herd’s mammary floor — especially valuable if expanding beyond Lux/Sapphire as mammary anchors. Sapphire already supplies elite dairy/width; she is not the mammary fix.

**What not to chase first this season:** Another tall/generalist buck without mammary proof; low-h² fine-tuning (rear leg set, feet details) ahead of MSL/TP; outside genetics that mainly duplicate Michael’s strength/RA package.
