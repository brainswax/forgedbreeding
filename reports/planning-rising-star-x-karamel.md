## Why Khaos scored so much better than her parents

Parents: Rising Star `VVE 88`, Karamel peak `VVEV 88` / latest `GVEV 87`. Daughter: **`VEEE 90`**.

### 1. Category balance — Mammary letter `E`

Karamel’s best card was Mammary `V`; Rising Star has no mammary letter. Khaos earned **Mammary `E`** plus Dairy Strength `E` and Body `E` with GA `V`. Final Score is category-driven: the same weak MSL/TP linears can still sit under an `E` mammary when **attachments, texture/capacity of the system, and udder depth** are elite. Her **FA 38 / RH 39 / RUA 44 / UD 17** package is why the letter (and thus FS) cleared 90 even with MS 17 / TP 18.

### 2. She combined the better frame of each role — and beat the midpoint on width

| Lever | Parental problem | What Khaos did |
|-------|------------------|----------------|
| Width | Dam 2024 **RW 21** would have capped the pair | Took sire width and went to **44** |
| Strength / depth | Dam 2024 STR 26 / BD 37 | Took sire **41 / 48** level (**40 / 49**) |
| Dairyness | Sire only 33 | Matched/beat dam trajectory → **38** |
| Rear udder | Sire blank | Kept/improved dam topography → arch **44** |

She is not an “average of 88 + 88.” She is **sire frame × dam dairy/udder**, with positive expression on width and dairyness.

### 3. Timing — compare the right dam card

If you only look at Karamel’s **2026** sheet (RW 35, STR 41, BD 48), Khaos looks like a slight polish on an already elite dam. The breeding was decided when Karamel’s **2024** card still showed **narrow width (21) and modest strength (26)**. Against *that* dam snapshot, Rising Star was a large complementary upgrade — and Khaos is the proof.

### 4. What did *not* get better (so FS gain is not a mammary-linear win)

MSL and TP are **as weak or weaker** than the dam. The +2 Final Score vs parental 88s is **not** “fixed suspensory/teats.” It is **elite everything around those faults** (capacity, width, dairy, attachments, shallow udder) producing a higher category Mammary letter and a cleaner overall card.

### 5. Sibling context (same sire, other dams)

Rising Star’s other typed daughters on the same sheet (Passion Project `GVEG 85`, RisingCrescentmoon `GGEA 82`) show the sire can throw mid-80s without a Khaos ceiling. **Karamel’s dairy/udder side + RS frame** is the plausible reason Khaos is the standout — not Rising Star alone.

## Breeding Implications / Recommendation

### How to use Khaos

1. **Protect** width (44), dairyness (38), capacity, RUA (44), shallow UD, and the `VEEE 90` balance.  
2. **Next buck must be a mammary specialist** — MSL and teat placement are the only binding gaps; Rising Star (or sons) will not fix them.

4. **Full sisters / same pair:** Rank on whether they kept Karamel’s dairy/udder *and* RS width; then require MSL/TP at freshening. Passion Project–class outcomes are the warning case.  
5. **Low-h²:** Protect arch; breed in better RLSV over generations.

### Bottom line

Rising Star × Karamel produced a daughter who **outscored both parents** by combining **his strength/depth/width** with **her dairy and rear-udder topography**, then adding favorable udder depth and an `E` mammary letter. She did **not** outscore them on MSL or teat placement — those stacked weak/wide. Treat Khaos as the pair’s **best-case frame/score outcome** and a clear map of what still needs outside mammary genetics.
