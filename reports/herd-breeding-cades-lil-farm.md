## Outside Genetics — Biggest Levers (for this set)

Binding near-term gaps across both does: **Medial Suspensory Ligament** (17–18) and **teat traits** (Mystique TP 18 / TD 20; Winnie TD 17 / TP 24), plus **RA above preferred** (38 / 36). In-set bucks are all estimated; White Water is the least-penalized mammary story on paper, not a proven fix.

**Outside buck (preferred)** — transmitting evidence with:
1. **MSL** clearly above intermediate — daughters/means roughly **≥28–32**
2. **Teat Placement** closer — roughly **≥25–30** in progeny; teat diameter not chronically fine
3. Hold **Dairyness** (≥32) and **Rump Width** (≥30) so Winnie’s ceiling traits are not the price of mammary
4. **Rump Angle** toward **30–35** (not a flattener for either doe)
5. Bonus (low-h²): strong **Rear Udder Arch** and intermediate **RLSV** so Winnie/Mystique protect traits survive
6. Prefer **progeny-proven mammary** over another unproven Aquata/Unlimited/Aladdin-line dairy-width sire

**Outside doe (optional):** Less urgent than a proven mammary sire for this two-doe set; if adding dams, prioritize strong MSL + closer teats without flat RA.

**What not to chase first inside this set:** Using **Cullasaja** or **Alaska** as mammary fixers for Mystique/Winnie — both trigger weak-MSL stack penalties and Alaska adds flat-RA caution. That is **sequencing within this custom matrix**, not a reason to ignore low-h² protect rules on keepers.
