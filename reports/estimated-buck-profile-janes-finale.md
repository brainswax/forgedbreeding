## Estimated Transmitting Profile

### Likely strengths (higher confidence)

- **Body capacity / overall score package** — Unlimited daughters routinely land Body E/V and Final Scores in the mid–high 80s; Unlimited himself is `VEE 90`. Finale should transmit capacity and competitive Final Score potential.
- **Dairyness** — Daughter mean DY ≈ 33 despite many dams; Unlimited’s own DY 33–34. Even with Jane Doe’s weak mature DY (22), sire-side dairy transmission looks strong. Expect Finale to be a dairyness improver relative to low-DY does.
- **Rump width** — Unlimited RW 29–30; daughter mean ≈ 30. Clear contrast to Jane Doe’s 15–25. Finale is more likely than average to help narrow-rumped does (and to buffer flatter angles via width).
- **Rear udder height / arch** — Daughter means RH ≈ 35, RUA ≈ 35. Useful mammary topography even when MSL/teats are only middling.

### Possible strengths (moderate confidence)

- **Strength** — Parental midparent ≈ 30–31; daughter mean ≈ 30. Adequate, not elite.
- **Mammary category letters** — Half of Unlimited daughters hit MMY E on their best appraisal. Finale may help Final Score via mammary *category* even when individual linears are imperfect — but this is less reliable than the frame/dairy signal.
- **Stature** — Unlimited is tall (33–37); Jane Doe variable (22–30). Offspring stature will depend heavily on the doe; Finale himself may land mid-to-upper miniature range. Treat as situational, not a guaranteed tall transmitter.

### Likely weaknesses or risks

- **Teat placement** — Unlimited daughters cluster around TP ≈ 21 (same as Jane Doe’s best mature score). Dam had TP 16–21. Finale is **unlikely** to be a teat-placement fixer; expect transmission near the low-20s unless the doe already brings better placement.
- **Medial suspensory (MSL)** — Daughter mean MS ≈ 24–25 is only intermediate. Jane Doe was 19–23. Finale may still *improve* Forged Farm does that sit at 13–18, but he should not be treated as an elite MSL buck. Risk of stacking soft MSL if used on the weakest-MSL does without other improvement.
- **Teat diameter / size** — Wide scatter in Unlimited daughters (TD 15–36), including several high-scoring does with very small teats (Hello Goodbye TD 15, Wedding Vows 18, Glimmer 17). Flag as a **watch item**, not a fixed fault.
- **Rump angle extremes** — Usually intermediate (~29), but outliers exist (Sweet Emotion RA 41; Lil Firecracker 36). Combined with Jane Doe’s 26–28, Finale’s own RA is probably intermediate; still avoid assuming he “corrects” flat or steep extremes.
- **Rear legs – side view** — Daughter mean ≈ 26; Unlimited 25–26. Soft RL SV can appear; low-h² — track, don’t over-weight for short-term selection.

- **Higher confidence:** Unlimited’s dairy/capacity/width transmitting pattern (24 daughters, consistent means, many 88–91).

- **Major gaps:** No Finale LA; no Jane Doe daughters in the reference file; teat-size inconsistency unexplained by dam alone.

When ranking Finale against **Michael Darling** (`VGE 88`) or **AX Smithy** (`VVE 86`), treat this profile as **directional only**. Real LA on Finale should replace these estimates.

## Benefit / Risk / Mitigation (herd use)

**Expected benefit:** Lift dairyness and rump width; support Body Capacity and Final Score; modest MSL improvement vs the current doe floor (13–18 → toward low–mid 20s if he transmits like Unlimited daughters).

**Main risks:** Soft teat placement stacking; only intermediate MSL; occasional small-teat or flatter-RA outliers in the Unlimited book.

**Mitigations:** Prefer partners that already bring better TP (and ideally stronger MSL); avoid using him as the sole “mammary fixer”; pair width-needy or low-DY does where his clearer strengths apply; re-evaluate after his own appraisal and first daughters.

**Package assessment:** Favorable as a **complementary third buck** for dairyness + width, not as the primary MSL/teat solution the herd still needs from outside or from a future higher-mammary sire.
