## Breeding Implications / Recommendation

### How to choose among daughters (priority order)

1. **Rump Width** — keep the widest; the narrowest sisters are the ones that lock in Smithy’s 24 on Sapphire’s line. Width is the trait this pair most risks losing.
2. **Dairyness** — prefer daughters that still look like Sapphire (elite dairy), not merely “better than average.” Cull-lean on coarse or only-moderate dairy kids.
3. **Rump Angle** — prefer movement toward **30–35**; still-steep is less ideal but **better than losing width** to chase a small angle change.
4. **After freshening (final keeper decision)**  
   - **Medial Suspensory Ligament** — require clear improvement over dam 17; weak cleft → do not retain as a herd doe.  
   - **Teat Placement** — prefer closer than dam 21; still-wide + weak MSL → hard pass.  
   - Protect **Rear Udder Height / Arch** and **Fore Udder Attachment** if present (arch is low-h² — once lost, slow to recover).  
   - Watch **Udder Depth** with support — deep + weak MSL is a longevity pass.
5. **Strength / Body Depth / RLSV** — tie-breakers only after width, dairy, and mammary.
6. **Do not keep a sister mainly because she is “prettier than average for the herd”** if she has already lost Sapphire’s width or dairy character — that is average-down selection.

### Practical keep / pass shorthand

| Keep if… | Pass / sell if… |
|----------|-----------------|
| Widest rumps in the sister set **and** dairy near the dam | Narrow rumps (Smithy-like) |
| Holds elite / near-elite dairyness | Moderate or coarse dairy vs Sapphire |
| At freshening: MSL and TP clearly better than dam | MSL still weak + teats still wide |
| Rear udder stays high/arched with strong fore attachment | Deep udder + weak medial |

### Use of keepers

- Best daughters: next breed to bucks that add **proven MSL and closer teat placement** without dumping dairyness — outside mammary genetics first. On-hand, **Michael** dilutes width less than repeating Smithy (BIS −2.4 vs −3.1); still not a mammary fix.
- **Do not breed keepers back to Smithy** if they already narrowed up — that stacks the dilution this report flags.
- Long-term: Sapphire’s line is a **dairyness / width / rear-udder anchor**. These daughters are keepers only if they **preserve that anchor** and **upgrade mammary**. Everything else is secondary.

### Bottom line

Opposite problem from Smithy × Snickers: here you **protect elite dam traits** (width + dairyness) while hoping mammary improves at freshening. Rank sisters **width → dairyness → angle**; lock herd spots only on **MSL and teat placement**. Most of this set should leave unless a sister clearly holds Sapphire and beats her mammary gaps.
